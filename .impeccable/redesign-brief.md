## Direction contract

THESIS: A photographic programme for student-built machines and the people behind them. Replace repetitive translucent cards with cinematic image framing, expressive typography, and varied editorial sections.

OWN-WORLD: Iron Bears orange, charcoal and pale neutral text; Barlow Condensed display with Manrope body; squared photographic frames, restrained control corners, fine rules, and strong typographic hierarchy. Respect the original logo and full-color sponsor marks. Native CSS and the existing Next.js/Tailwind stack, with a single icon family.

STORY: See the robot competing; understand that students build it; explore engineering, coding and community; browse real seasons; recognize supporters; find a clear route to contact the team.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact horizontal wordmark/navigation header above a large competition photograph, with a left-aligned two-line headline, concise supporting text, and visible team/sponsor actions. Orange display emphasis and a discrete three-frame photographic selector make the first screen memorable. The robot remains large and recognizable. Mobile keeps the headline, action, and photograph legible without overlapping navigation.

FORM: Independent film festival programme, candidate 7, seed 211aeff0. Signature interaction: select Compete, Build, or Connect to change the documentary hero image and caption. History adds a working era filter and a featured latest season. Motion: one short image reveal/crossfade, restrained hover feedback, and reduced-motion support. Dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 8, MOTION_INTENSITY 5, VISUAL_DENSITY 4.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
